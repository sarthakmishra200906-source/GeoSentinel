# GeoSentinel Basic Safety FAQ

This local starter document contains general safety guidance. It is not a
replacement for an official order. Admins should upload and approve a verified
government source before using jurisdiction-specific instructions.

## What are basic disaster safety measures?

Move away from immediate hazards, stay with your group, keep your phone
charged, carry essential medicines and documents, and follow instructions from
local authorities. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.

## What is the evacuation process?

Follow an official evacuation order, leave by the recommended route, take
essential medicines and documents, avoid floodwater and damaged bridges, help
children and people needing assistance, and report to the designated safe
location. Do not return until authorities say it is safe.

## What should I do around floodwater?

Do not walk or drive through moving or unknown-depth floodwater. Move to higher
ground if it is safe, avoid downed wires, and contact local emergency services
if you are trapped or in immediate danger.
